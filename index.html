Discover the wholesome power of ancient grains. Discover Our Mission

About Us

Enhancing the visibility and acceptance of millet-based products across rural and urban communities. We are dedicated to building a sustainable ecosystem that connects growers, processors, and health-conscious consumers.

Villupuram

Millets are packed with B-vitamins, calcium, iron, and magnesium. Naturally gluten-free with a low glycemic index, they are ideal for healthy lifestyles and diabetic care.

Jawadhar Hills

Supporting eco-friendly farming practices and empowering small-scale enterprises through advanced food processing and value-added millet creations.

Dharmapuri

Reviving ancient grains like Ragi, Bajra, Thinai, and Samai by bringing wholesome, traditional recipes into modern kitchens with a creative touch.
