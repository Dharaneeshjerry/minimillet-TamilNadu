DST-SHRI Funded Academic Project

Reviving Ancient Grains of Tamil Nadu

Millet Tamil Nadu is an academic initiative from Rajalakshmi Engineering College documenting the state's millet heritage — from Villupuram's plains to the tribal belts of Jawadhar Hills and the red-soil farms of Dharmapuri. Explore the nutritional science, regional cultivation, traditional recipes, and the processing technology bringing these grains back to modern kitchens.

DST-SHRI Funded Project

Digitally Fusing the Traditional Knowledge Gap

To foster the potential use of millets as a nutritious alternative to mitigate micronutrient deficiencies

DST-SHRI

🟢 On-going
📋 Expected Outcomes
01E-Library (web & mobile app) — a repository on knowledge base of traditional millet formulations
02Technology knowhow for millet-based products to be distributed to younger generations to mitigate micronutrient deficiencies
03Pilot scale equipment facility for budding entrepreneurs and SHGs
